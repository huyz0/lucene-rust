# lucene-vector-quant, lucene-ivf -- IVF vector index with pluggable quantizers

[Index](../parity.md). Not a Lucene module: an inverted-file (cluster-based)
approximate nearest-neighbour index of this port's own design, for local and
blob storage. Stock Lucene and OpenSearch cannot read its files; it is not
wired into a codec or the OpenSearch plugin. The quantizers reuse Lucene's
(`OptimizedScalarQuantizer`, `ScalarQuantizer`). Benchmarks:
`crates/lucene-ivf/examples/ivf_bench.rs`, `benchmarks/ivf/`.

Object-safe quantizer trait: fixed-length codes encoded relative to a centroid, a query scorer re-targeted per partition and scoring a block of codes per call, centroids optionally mapped once into the quantizer's own space (`prepare_centroid`, `encode_prepared`, `set_prepared_centroid`), state written by name and read back through a registry (`osq`, `sq`, `erq`, `rot-osq`, `rot-sq` built in). `QueryScorer::error_bounds` (default: unknown) gives each estimate a margin in score space, and `score_margin` maps a raw inner-product or squared-distance error to one; probabilistic margins span `BOUND_CONFIDENCE` (1.9, RaBitQ's `epsilon_0`) deviations. Every `ScalarEncoding` (BBQ 1-bit/4-bit query, 2, 4, 7, 8 bits) with Lucene104's record layout and corrected score, encoded per cluster centroid. Differs: dimensions padded to a multiple of 64 at 4 bits and under; 1- and 2-bit codes are stored 32 to a block, interleaved by groups of four dimensions, and scored by fast scan (16-entry tables in SIMD registers, `lucene-util::simd::lut16_accumulate32`), with the same integer dot product and corrected score; the query interval is not refined (one quantization per probed cluster) and its mean/variance are plain sums. Error margin: a deviation of `sqrt((\|y\| lx / 2)^2 + (max(\|lower\|, \|upper\|) \|e\|)^2)` on the residuals' inner product, from the document's step `lx` (half a step per dimension) and the query's exact quantization error `e`. Tests: estimate error per encoding and similarity; state round trip. |
| -- (Extended RaBitQ, Gao et al., "Practical and Asymptotically Optimal Quantization of High-Dimensional Vectors in Euclidean Space for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search", SIGMOD 2025) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rabitq.rs::{ExRabitqFactory, ExRabitqQuantizer, NAME, DEFAULT_SEED}` | **rust-only**. 2..=8 bits per dimension, per cluster centroid: vectors and centroids rotated by `rotation::Rotation` (3 rounds; dimension padded to a power of two, at least 8), each centroid once (`prepare_centroid`); code chosen by the paper's critical-value search for the rescaling factor of largest cosine (critical values bucket-sorted, limited per bit width to a window around the factor that saturates the largest coordinate), unbiased inner-product estimate `\|r\|^2 <y, P x> / <y, P r>` with three corrective floats. Up to 6 bits codes are bit planes stored 32 to a block and scored by fast scan (`lut16_accumulate32`, one pass per plane) against a 6-bit query; 7 and 8 bits are a byte per dimension scored by a byte dot product against an 8-bit query; the codebook offset is applied to the quantized query. State is bit width and seed. Error margin: RaBitQ's bound, `\|r\| sqrt((tan(theta) \|z\|)^2 / n + step^2 / (12 cos(theta)^2))` deviations with `tan(theta)` at a per-bit-width ceiling measured over rotated residuals (0.5 at 2 bits to 0.012 at 8) and `\|r\|` from the stored terms; unknown for `MaximumInnerProduct`. Tests: estimate error falls with bits and is at or near OSQ's at 2, 4, 7, 8 bits on isotropic data and far below on skewed data, unbiasedness over rotations, all similarities at dimensions 1, 7, 64, 100, 300, blocks scoring as single codes, prepared and plain centroids agreeing, search window against the full search; `tests/ivf.rs` recall. |
| -- (RaBitQ's random rotation, Gao & Long 2024) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rotation.rs::{RotatedFactory, RotatedQuantizer, Rotation, fwht, PREFIX}` | **rust-only**. Wraps any quantizer factory as `rot-<inner name>` (`rot-osq`, `rot-sq` built in): vectors, centroids, queries and the training sample are zero-padded to the next power of two and rotated by a seeded randomized Hadamard transform (sign flips then fast Walsh-Hadamard, 3 rounds by default), which keeps every similarity's score; the inner quantizer works at the padded dimension and its codes, blocks and scores are used as they are. `prepare_centroid` returns the rotated centroid, which the IVF writer computes once per cluster and the reader once per cluster at open, so neither encoding nor probing rotates a centroid. State: seed, rounds, inner state. Tests: orthogonality for dims 1 to 300, determinism per seed, state round trip and corrupt states, estimate error, rotated BBQ against plain BBQ on skewed data, error by round count, prepared and plain centroids agreeing; `tests/ivf.rs` recall. |

| -- | `lucene-ivf/src/writer.rs::{IvfWriter, IvfParams, FieldVectors, IvfFiles}` | **rust-only**. | -- | `lucene-ivf/src/calibrate.rs::{Calibration, DEFAULT_CALIBRATION_RECALL, MULTIPLES}` | **rust-only**. At write (so also at merge), 64 sampled vectors of the field (fewer in smaller fields) are searched against the field's own clusters and codes with themselves excluded, their exact top 10 found by brute force: nprobe is the fewest clusters (1, 2, 4, ..., all) holding `(1 + target) / 2` of the true neighbours, rerank the fewest candidates per hit (1 to 50) reaching `target` (default 0.95) recall at that nprobe; stored as a fraction of the clusters and a multiple of `k`. Deterministic; parallel over queries; measured on GloVe-100 (390k) as within run-to-run noise of the build time. Tests: `tests/dynamic_rerank.rs` (determinism, target monotonicity, tiny and spilled fields, fallback without calibration). |
| -- | `lucene-ivf/src/reader.rs::{IvfReader, IvfField, RangeSource, SearchParams, ScoredDoc}` | **rust-only**. Opens with header, footer-shape and bounds checks (full CRC in `check_integrity`); keeps each centroid as the quantizer prepares it (`Quantizer::prepare_centroid`) beside the original when the two differ; search ranks centroids, scans `nprobe` clusters' codes with an optional filter, reranks with exact scores; exact search for selective filters. `SearchParams` takes `Probes::{Fixed, Auto}` and `Rerank::{Fixed, Auto, Bounded}`: fixed modes rerank exactly the best estimates; `Auto` uses the field's calibration (defaults `DEFAULT_PROBE_FRACTION`, `DEFAULT_RERANK_MULTIPLE` without one), and `Auto`/`Bounded` rerank in estimate order, stopping once the `k`-th exact score beats every remaining estimate plus its error margin and skipping candidates that cannot beat it. `search_with` reports `SearchStats` and shares a `SharedThreshold` (atomic, monotone `k`-th exact score) across segments: a probed cluster is skipped when its radius bound (`max(0, \|q - m\| - r)` distance, `<q, m> + \|q\| r` inner product) is below it; `segment_nprobe` splits a probe budget by segment size. Measured (GloVe-100 390k, cosine, 1000 queries, calibrated nprobe 129 of 1693): erq4 fixed rerank 300 recall 0.9833, `Bounded{cap: 300}` 0.9832 with 19.1 reranked per query, `Auto` (cap 15) 0.9765 with 14.4; BBQ fixed 300 0.9551, `Auto` (calibrated cap 300) 0.9548 with 263.5. Tests: recall over 4 similarities and 10 quantizers, filters, corruption, copying and unaligned range sources; `tests/dynamic_rerank.rs`: fixed rerank against a manual exact rerank, `Bounded` with unknown or infinite margins equal to `Fixed`, early stop with sound margins keeping the hits, `Auto` recall and work against deep fixed rerank, radii covering every member, shared thresholds giving the merged top `k` of independent searches, version 2 files, corrupt radii and calibration. |
