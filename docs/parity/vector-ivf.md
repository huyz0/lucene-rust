# lucene-vector-quant, lucene-ivf -- IVF vector index with pluggable quantizers

[Index](../parity.md). Not a Lucene module: an inverted-file (cluster-based)
approximate nearest-neighbour index of this port's own design, for local and
blob storage. Stock Lucene and OpenSearch cannot read its files; it is not
wired into a codec or the OpenSearch plugin. The quantizers reuse Lucene's
(`OptimizedScalarQuantizer`, `ScalarQuantizer`). Benchmarks:
`crates/lucene-ivf/examples/ivf_bench.rs`, `benchmarks/ivf/`.

| -- (Extended RaBitQ, Gao et al., "Practical and Asymptotically Optimal Quantization of High-Dimensional Vectors in Euclidean Space for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search", SIGMOD 2025) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rabitq.rs::{ExRabitqFactory, ExRabitqQuantizer, NAME, DEFAULT_SEED}` | **rust-only**. 2..=8 bits per dimension, per cluster centroid: vectors and centroids rotated by `rotation::Rotation` (3 rounds; dimension padded to a power of two, at least 8), each centroid once (`prepare_centroid`); code chosen by the paper's critical-value search for the rescaling factor of largest cosine (critical values bucket-sorted, limited per bit width to a window around the factor that saturates the largest coordinate), unbiased inner-product estimate `\|r\|^2 <y, P x> / <y, P r>` with three corrective floats. Up to 6 bits codes are bit planes stored 32 to a block and scored by fast scan (`lut16_accumulate32`, one pass per plane) against a 6-bit query; 7 and 8 bits are a byte per dimension scored by a byte dot product against an 8-bit query; the codebook offset is applied to the quantized query. State is bit width and seed. Tests: estimate error falls with bits and is at or near OSQ's at 2, 4, 7, 8 bits on isotropic data and far below on skewed data, unbiasedness over rotations, all similarities at dimensions 1, 7, 64, 100, 300, blocks scoring as single codes, prepared and plain centroids agreeing, search window against the full search; `tests/ivf.rs` recall. |
| -- (RaBitQ's random rotation, Gao & Long 2024) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rotation.rs::{RotatedFactory, RotatedQuantizer, Rotation, fwht, PREFIX}` | **rust-only**. Wraps any quantizer factory as `rot-<inner name>` (`rot-osq`, `rot-sq` built in): vectors, centroids, queries and the training sample are zero-padded to the next power of two and rotated by a seeded randomized Hadamard transform (sign flips then fast Walsh-Hadamard, 3 rounds by default), which keeps every similarity's score; the inner quantizer works at the padded dimension and its codes, blocks and scores are used as they are. `prepare_centroid` returns the rotated centroid, which the IVF writer computes once per cluster and the reader once per cluster at open, so neither encoding nor probing rotates a centroid. State: seed, rounds, inner state. Tests: orthogonality for dims 1 to 300, determinism per seed, state round trip and corrupt states, estimate error, rotated BBQ against plain BBQ on skewed data, error by round count, prepared and plain centroids agreeing; `tests/ivf.rs` recall. |
