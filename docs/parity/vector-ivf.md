# lucene-vector-quant, lucene-ivf -- IVF vector index with pluggable quantizers

[Index](../parity.md). Not a Lucene module: an inverted-file (cluster-based)
approximate nearest-neighbour index of this port's own design, for local and
blob storage. Stock Lucene and OpenSearch cannot read its files; it is not
wired into a codec or the OpenSearch plugin. The quantizers reuse Lucene's
(`OptimizedScalarQuantizer`, `ScalarQuantizer`). Benchmarks:
`crates/lucene-ivf/examples/ivf_bench.rs`, `benchmarks/ivf/`.

| -- (Extended RaBitQ, Gao et al., "Practical and Asymptotically Optimal Quantization of High-Dimensional Vectors in Euclidean Space for Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search", SIGMOD 2025) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rabitq.rs::{ExRabitqFactory, ExRabitqQuantizer, NAME, DEFAULT_SEED}` | **rust-only**. 2..=8 bits per dimension, per cluster centroid: vectors and centroids rotated by `rotation::Rotation` (3 rounds) into the code dimension: the dimension padded to a multiple of 32 (to a power of two, at least 8, up to 32), each centroid once (`prepare_centroid`); code chosen by the paper's critical-value search for the rescaling factor of largest cosine (critical values bucket-sorted, limited per bit width to a window around the factor that saturates the largest coordinate), unbiased inner-product estimate `\|r\|^2 <y, P x> / <y, P r>` with three corrective floats. | -- (RaBitQ's random rotation, Gao & Long 2024) | `lucene-vector-quant/src/rotation.rs::{RotatedFactory, RotatedQuantizer, Rotation, fwht, max_width, PREFIX}` | **rust-only**. Wraps any quantizer factory as `rot-<inner name>` (`rot-osq`, `rot-sq` built in): vectors, centroids, queries and the training sample are zero-padded to the inner factory's code dimension (`QuantizerFactory::code_dimension`: a multiple of 64 for OSQ at 4 bits and under, even for int4, the dimension itself otherwise) and rotated there, so the rotation adds no code bytes and spreads energy into padding the codes carry anyway. The rotation at width `m` is a seeded randomized Hadamard transform, 3 rounds by default; with `b` the largest power of two at most `m`, a round is a sign flip and a scaled fast Walsh-Hadamard transform on `[0, b)`, and when `m` is not a power of two a random permutation first and a second sign flip and transform on the overlapping block `[m - b, m)`: exactly orthogonal, `O(m log m)`, and a unit axis spreads about as under a random rotation (largest coordinate under `6/sqrt(m)`). This keeps every similarity's score; the inner quantizer works at the code dimension and its codes, blocks and scores are used as they are. `prepare_centroid` returns the rotated centroid, which the IVF writer computes once per cluster and the reader once per cluster at open, so neither encoding nor probing rotates a centroid. State: seed, rounds, width (`u32`, checked against the dimension: `dimension..=max(2 * dimension, 64)`), inner state. Tests: orthogonality for dims 1 to 768 at the dimension, the next multiple of 64 and the next power of two, axis spreading, code lengths equal to the inner quantizer's for every built-in inner quantizer, determinism per seed, state round trip and corrupt states, estimate error, rotated BBQ against plain BBQ on skewed data, error by round count, prepared and plain centroids agreeing; `tests/ivf.rs` recall. |

| -- (SOAR, Sun et al. 2023; SPFresh, Xu et al. 2023, for `reuse`) | `lucene-ivf/src/kmeans.rs::{cluster, reuse, soar_assign, KMeansParams, Clustering, NO_CLUSTER, REUSE_SPLIT_FACTOR, REUSE_DISSOLVE_DIVISOR}` | **rust-only**. Hierarchical k-means (splits of at most 64, sampled training) to clusters of a target size, then neighbourhood Lloyd refinement and full Lloyd rounds (every vector against every centroid, register-tiled kernel); optional SOAR spilling. `reuse` starts from an existing partition instead: kept vectors stay in their cluster (or the nearer of the two holding a spilled one), new ones go to their nearest centroid (full-assignment kernel), then neighbourhood rounds over the clusters that received vectors and their neighbours, clusters under a quarter of the target dissolved into their nearest remaining one, clusters over twice the target split by the same sampled k-means; no full Lloyd round. Deterministic per seed. Measured: on GloVe-100 spilling gave no recall gain per entry scanned, so it is off by default; two full rounds raise recall at nprobe 64 from 0.949 to 0.955. Tests: unit tests, full assignment against brute force, `reuse` keeping, placing, splitting, dissolving and its fallbacks. |

| -- | `lucene-ivf/src/merge.rs::{merge_field, MergeSource, MergeClustering}` | **rust-only**. Merge decodes the live vectors from any source encoding and re-quantizes them under their new document numbers. Clusters per `IvfParams::merge_clustering`: `Recluster` runs the full k-means; `ReuseLargest { min_fraction, refine_rounds }` (default 0.5, 1) reuses the centroids of the source with the most live vectors when it holds at least `min_fraction` of them and has two or more clusters (`kmeans::reuse`; each of its slots' primary cluster from the recorded own counts with int8, the cluster ranges without spilling, otherwise the one or two clusters whose postings hold it), and falls back to `Recluster` otherwise. Tests: `merge_reclusters_live_vectors_under_new_numbers`, `merge_decodes_every_encoding`, `merge_reuses_the_largest_source_only_when_it_dominates`, `reusing_merge_recalls_like_reclustering` (all similarities, deletions, determinism), `reusing_merge_splits_and_dissolves`, `reusing_merge_reads_spilled_sources`, `reusing_merge_rejects_postings_that_lose_or_repeat_slots`.
