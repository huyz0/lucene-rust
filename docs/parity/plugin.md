## M10 T10.7: joins, spans, intervals, combined fields, function score

OpenSearch 3.9.0 shapes: **native** (encoded by `ExtendedEncoder`)
or **falls back** (named reason). Proved by `NativeSelfTestM10` (hits, score
bits, counts, sorts, aggregations vs Lucene), `e2e/m10_matrix.py`.
