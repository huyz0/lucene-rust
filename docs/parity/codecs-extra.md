# lucene-codecs (M14.g)

[Index](../parity.md). `org.apache.lucene.codecs.*` in the `lucene-codecs` jar: the non-default
postings formats and codecs. The default codec's formats are in [codecs.md](codecs.md); the
class-by-class triage is `docs/inventory/lucene-codecs.tsv`. These formats are module-level readers
and writers over a group's files, like sandbox's `IDVersion` ([sandbox.md](sandbox.md)): this
port's `IndexWriter` routes no field to them and `DirectoryReader` does not open them. Unit tests
without a Java counterpart: `lucene-codecs/src/bloom/tests.rs`, `lucene-codecs/src/memory/tests.rs`.

| `index/{SegmentWriteState, SegmentReadState}` | `lucene-codecs/src/segment_state.rs::{SegmentWriteState, SegmentReadState}` | **ported** M14.g: what a wrapping postings format is constructed with -- `maxDoc`, the segment id, the codec suffix, the whole segment's `FieldInfos` (and from it `hasProx`/`hasPayloads || hasOffsets`, which decide `Lucene104PostingsWriter`'s `.pos`/`.pay`); the carrier for every M14.g postings format. Java's `.blm`s hold one entry each: a multi-entry one is proven only by Lucene reading Rust's. Differs: entries in field-number order (Java: `HashMap` identity order); the delegate must be `Lucene104`; `check_blm_footer` checks the `.blm` alone. Gap: only `try_seek_exact` goes through the filter (`iter`, `intersect`, `seekCeil`, `ord` are the delegate's); no `prepareSeekExact`/`has*`. Bench (`bench-micro.sh codecs`, 200,000 lookups, a quarter present): `cx_bloom_seek` 1.07x; the write side is not benchmarked. |
| `codecs/bloom/FuzzySet` | `lucene-codecs/src/bloom/fuzzy_set.rs::{FuzzySet, ContainsResult, get_nearest_set_size, get_nearest_set_size_for_saturation, get_estimated_number_unique_values_allowing_for_collisions}` | **ported** M14.g: power-of-two sizes, the three `createSet*` (Java's `Math.log`/`pow` through `strict_math`, `float` rounding), `murmurhash3_x64_128` positions, `downsize`, the unique-value estimate, `serialize`/`deserialize`.
