# lucene-disk-hnsw -- HNSW on disk, one read per hop

[Index](../parity.md). Not a Lucene module: an opt-in on-disk layout for
the stock HNSW graph, after DiskANN (Subramanya et al., NeurIPS 2019) and
LM-DiskANN (Pan et al., 2023), for local and blob storage. Stock Lucene and
OpenSearch cannot read its files; the stock `Lucene99HnswVectorsFormat`
stays the default and is unchanged, and a stock segment's graph converts
without a rebuild. Not wired into a codec or the OpenSearch plugin.
Benchmark: `crates/lucene-disk-hnsw/examples/disk_hnsw_bench.rs`,
results in `docs/benchmarks/disk-hnsw.md`.

A record is document, neighbour count, vector (`lucene-ivf`'s `VectorEncoding`: f32, f16, bf16, int8 from the field mean), `2 * M` neighbour slots and, with inline codes, the neighbours' codes; padded to 4 bytes, packed whole into pages (4096 by default), a record longer than a page taking whole pages; field regions start on a page boundary. Tests: unit tests (packing, spanning records, overflow refused). |
| `codecs.lucene99.Lucene99HnswVectorsWriter` (graph source) | `lucene-disk-hnsw/src/writer.rs::{DiskHnswWriter, DiskHnswParams, FieldVectors, DiskHnswFiles}` | **rust-only**. `add_field` builds the graph, `add_field_with_graph` takes any `HnswGraphView` (a stock `OffHeapHnswGraph` writes the same bytes as the on-heap graph it came from: neighbour lists are stored ascending, as `.vex` keeps them). Cosine vectors are stored unit length; the quantizer is trained on a strided sample and encodes against the field mean; upper levels and their codes go to `.dhm`. Tests: stock `.vex` conversion byte-identical, bad fields refused, several fields and an empty one. |
| `util.hnsw.HnswGraphSearcher` (descent) | `lucene-disk-hnsw/src/reader.rs::{DiskHnswReader, DiskHnswField, OpenOptions, SearchParams, SearchStats, SearchOutput, ScoredDoc}` | **rust-only**. Open checks headers, footers, versions, regions and upper-level structure (CRC of the paged files in `check_integrity`). Search descends the upper levels greedily on codes, then runs DiskANN's beam search on level 0: the best `beam_width` unexpanded of `list_len` candidates are announced with `RangeSource::prefetch` and read together, each expanded node is scored exactly from its record and then ranks by that score, neighbours enter on their code estimates (resident codes, CPU-prefetched, or inline codes from the record); hits are the best exact scores; filtered-out nodes are still traversed and do not take places on the list. Non-finite queries are refused. Tests: recall over 4 similarities and 4 quantizers with resident and inline codes, every vector encoding, filters, one read and prefetch per expanded node, round trips by beam width, corrupt records and files. |
